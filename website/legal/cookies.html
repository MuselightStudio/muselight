Cookie Policy

Last updated: [Date you publish this]

Muselight only uses cookies and similar storage (such as your browser's local storage) that are strictly necessary for the website and app to work. Because of that, we don't need to ask for your consent and you won't see a cookie banner.

What we use

WhatWhyHow long
Sign-in session (Supabase)Keeps you signed in to the web appUntil you sign out, or about a week of inactivity
PreferencesRemembers choices like your themeUntil you clear them

Security (Cloudflare)Protects the site from attacks and botsUp to 30 minutes, or as set by Cloudflare

No tracking or advertising

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics that track you across websites. If we ever add analytics that need consent, we'll ask you first and update this page.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, but the web app won't be able to keep you signed in without them.
