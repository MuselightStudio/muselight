Muselight for Windows

The full studio: storyboards, timeline, marketing, workflows and automations, plus the option to use your own provider accounts, ComfyUI and GPUs.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · about 35 MB · free to download, you pay only for what you create

Install

  1. Open Muselight.exe once it has downloaded.
  2. It runs straight away: there's nothing to install.
  3. Move it somewhere permanent, like your Desktop or Documents, and pin it to the taskbar.

"Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows this for new apps it hasn't seen many people use yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. It's only shown the first time.

What you need

Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome installed (Muselight opens in its own window using it) and an internet connection.

Not on Windows? Use Muselight in your browser: it works on any device.
