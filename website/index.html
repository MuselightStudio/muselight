Features

A whole production studio,
not just a prompt box

Everything you need to go from an idea to a finished image, ad or film, with every model a click away.

Always the cheapest provider

Connect fal.ai, WaveSpeedAI, Runware, Replicate and 20 more. Each run goes to whichever has the model cheapest, and you see every price first.

A prompt builder anyone can use

Pick real cameras, lenses, film stocks, lighting and camera moves in plain words, or one-click looks. A live checklist shows what your prompt is missing.

Storyboards and timeline

Plan a film scene by scene, make keyframes, animate them, then cut, trim and score everything into one video.

Workflows you can wire up

Chain models in a visual node editor: a prompt into a picture, into a video, into a soundtrack. Run it once or for a whole list.

Characters that stay themselves

Save people, products, places and brand kits to your Library, then type @name in any prompt to keep them consistent.

An assistant that does it for you

Describe what you want and the assistant picks the model, writes the prompt and runs it. Works with a free OpenRouter key.

30+ one-click tools

Upscale, remove backgrounds, relight, try new camera angles, change outfits, animate, or add music, subtitles or a dub to any result.

Batch and compare

Make dozens of variations from a list, or send one idea to several models side by side to find the best.

Your own GPUs

Run your ComfyUI workflows free on your own PC, or on RunPod GPUs you control. Voice, music and sound effects are built in too.

How it works

From download to your first creation in a few minutes

Download and open

Muselight is a single file. Double-click it: there's nothing to install and no account to make.

Add a provider key

fal.ai has the most models: sign up, add a little credit and paste your key. Add more providers any time to find cheaper prices.

Create anything

Pick a model, describe your idea and press Run. Every price is shown before you spend a cent, and you pay the provider directly.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free, with every feature. You only pay the AI providers for what you make, at their own prices, from your own accounts with them.

Which providers can I use?

fal.ai, WaveSpeedAI, Atlas Cloud, Runware, Replicate, Kie.ai, PiAPI, Segmind, DeepInfra, Together, SiliconFlow and Hugging Face, plus model makers like Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs, Kling, Runway, MiniMax and Luma, and your own GPUs through ComfyUI and RunPod. You need at least one; fal.ai is the best start.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is it safe?

The warning appears because the app isn't code-signed (a certificate costs hundreds a year). Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source code is on GitHub if you'd like to check it, or to build the app yourself.

Does Muselight see my prompts or files?

No. The app runs on your PC and sends each request straight to the provider you choose, under that provider's own terms. Nothing passes through us.

Who owns what I create?

You do, as far as the law and each model's own terms allow. Some models have their own usage terms; check them with the provider.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Muselight runs on Windows 10 and 11.
